THE ESSENTIALS

Bring your own
atmosphere.

01

Your media, locally

Import MP4 and MOV files directly from your Mac. No accounts, uploads, gallery subscriptions, or tracking.

02

Every display, in sync

A shared AVFoundation playback pipeline renders your chosen scene across connected displays.

03

Knows when to rest

Playback pauses around sleep, Low Power Mode, fullscreen work, and display changes—then resumes when appropriate.
